THE SERVICE COLLECTION

What does your
project need to do?

Four different jobs, with outputs you can combine. Choose by the decision and the deliverable; the numbers are not an accuracy ranking.

01 / OBSERVATION

360° VIDEO & IMAGERY

A site visit.
Available for replay.

Give stakeholders a shared view of visible conditions. Organized 360° capture lets teams revisit spaces, discuss issues, and document progress without coordinating another group visit.

What you receive

  • Organized visual walkthrough
  • Dated capture with area references
  • Observations tied to visible conditions

Where it fits

  • Remote coordination
  • Progress documentation
  • Facility orientation

Define up front Areas, route, capture dates and viewing format. Visual documentation alone does not establish measurement accuracy.

Add this service to your brief ↗
02 / EXISTING GEOMETRY

MOBILE MAPPING

Existing conditions.
Ready to model.

Capture the geometry of agreed spaces and provide a registered point cloud for use in Revit. Give the modeling team a spatial reference with documented coverage and known limitations.

What you receive

  • Registered point cloud
  • Agreed file format and reference
  • Coverage and limitation notes

Where it fits

  • Renovation planning
  • Existing-condition modeling
  • Facility coordination

Define up front Required features, dimensional tolerances, access and modeling responsibility. Revit model creation is scoped separately.

Add this service to your brief ↗
03 / SITE CONTEXT

GNSS + PHOTOGRAMMETRY

Understand the terrain.
Compare the possibilities.

Connect visible site conditions to a consistent coordinate framework. GNSS-supported photogrammetry produces imagery and surfaces for planning, terrain interpretation and quantity analysis.

What you receive

  • Georeferenced imagery
  • Point clouds and site surfaces
  • Accuracy checks and coverage notes

Where it fits

  • Site alternatives
  • Terrain analysis
  • Earthwork quantities

Define up front Control, coordinate framework, required accuracy and independent checkpoints. Visible surfaces are not automatically bare earth; obscured areas may need supplemental capture.

Add this service to your brief ↗
04 / CONTROL & VERIFICATION

TOTAL STATION + LAYERED LIDAR

Critical connections.
Verified geometry.

Combine total station control with complementary LiDAR scans where dimensional confidence governs the work. Plan capture and independent checks around the agreed tolerances and deliverables.

What you receive

  • Survey-controlled point clouds
  • Verified geometry and features
  • Documented checks and limitations

Where it fits

  • Detailed design inputs
  • Critical clearances
  • Existing-to-new connections

Define up front Critical dimensions, tolerances, control and independent verification. Survey-grade results depend on the complete workflow, not the equipment label.
